## Proposed research gap (hypothesis, not uniqueness claim)

An event-matched **YouTube metadata-focused** user pilot might help test how evidence-linked explanations, reviewed claim histories and labels affect accuracy judgments and simulated sharing intentions across a *defined small sample*. This is a proposed evaluation configuration. Similar interventions exist: the contribution would depend on actual controlled comparisons, independent annotations, documented methodology and observed results, none of which can be asserted yet.

## Minimum review work before paper submission

1. Verify each complete bibliography and the study-specific result in the original article, not only DOI metadata.
2. Identify closer studies on video claim verification, headline framing, cross-country comparisons and source labels. Record inclusion/search criteria, not just five convenient references.
3. Verify each paper's population, platform, design and boundary conditions before calling it a baseline.
4. Align RQ1–RQ5 and any hypotheses with actual collected outcomes. If no participants are available, present a technical feasibility report rather than an impact study.
5. Keep the exact source link and provenance for each A/B/C reference. Do not convert source-level external ratings into verified video-level conclusions.
